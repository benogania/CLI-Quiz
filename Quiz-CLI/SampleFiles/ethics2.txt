Happiness - reward for a religious life by an afterlife in paradise 
ultimate value of religious people 

Peace - basic condition for freedom and happiness 
not just the absence of war 

Love - feelings or experience of deep connectedness or oneness with another human being, any animal, plant, tree or unnamable
experience of something far beyond any comprehension and totally indescribable 
may happen when one opens to the beauty and nature of other people or to the beauty and mystery of nature in general or even beautiful things 

Freedom - experience of the unrestricted
independent of the social pressure of another 
related with empathy, integrity, and respect 

Safety - free of threat, fear and survival-stress 
freedom form emotional fear, helplessness and anxiety 

Intelligence - capacity for logic, understanding, selfawareness, learning, emotional knowledge, reasoning, planning, creativity and problem solving 
ability to perceive or infer information and retain it as knowledge to be applied towards adaptive behaviors within an environment or context
