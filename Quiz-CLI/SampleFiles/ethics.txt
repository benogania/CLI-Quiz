Respect - comes basically out of the understanding that deep down the other person is the same as we are ourselves 
the most basic principle of any social communication coming out of our perception, empathy and awareness that the other is basically as we are ourselves 

Equality - originated from old French or Latin words as aequalis, aequus, aequalitas (even, level, equal) 25. right to claim equal liberties with others
absence of discrimination

Justice - proper administration of the law 
fair and equitable treatment of all individuals under the law 
needed to realize and maintain our highest human values of freedom, peace, love and happiness 

Nature - understanding our physical dependence of nature 
awareness of being a part of the whole system of the created world 

Health - state of complete physical, mental and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity 
resource for everyday life not the objective of living 
positive concept emphasizing social and personal resources as well as physical capacities
